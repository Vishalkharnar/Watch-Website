    Extreme Engineering

    Mechanical Components

    01.

    Carbon TPT® Bezel

    The outer bezel is milled from sheets of carbon fibers obtained by separating thread systems. Alternating layers are woven at a 45° angle, then heated to 120°C in an autoclave to build exceptional shock resistance.

    Thickness1.40 mm
    ScrewsTitanium Grade 5 spline
    02.

    Sapphire Dial Plate

    Cut from a single crystal block, this dial is treated with double-sided anti-reflective coatings. The numbers and sub-dials are printed using specialized industrial techniques to sit hovering above the movements.

    MaterialSynthetic Sapphire
    Hardness9 Mohs scale
    03.

    Caliber MX-01 Movement

    The heartbeat. A manual-winding skeleton tourbillon caliber with hours, minutes, and power-reserve indicator. Features grade 5 titanium plates and bridges with black PVD finishing, keeping the entire caliber under 7 grams.

    Jewels38 Rubies
    Power Reserve70 Hours
    04.

    TPT Caseback Chassis

    The base anchor. Engineered with spline screws to hold the front bezel and dial plate in hermetic tension. Fitted with Nitrile O-ring seals to ensure water resistance up to 50 meters.

    SealsDouble Nitrile O-ring
    Water Resistance50m / 5 ATM
